What the radar keeps

A protocol is a launch candidate when the snapshot records no token for it (no gecko id, no symbol, no market cap) and it is not a product of something that already trades. Products are removed four ways, and the counts are the snapshot's own:

    DefiLlama's record is evidence, not proof: the board says a protocol records no token, never that it has none. A name the record keeps may still have a token the record does not carry.

    How a launch is valued

    implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity

    • Category multiple — the median market-cap-to-TVL of the launched protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the market-wide median (a prior of 8) and winsorised to 0.02–3.0, the range the launched market actually shows.
    • Momentum — three quarters of the seven-day TVL change, clamped to 0.55–1.75, so one wild week cannot rule a valuation.
    • Maturity — 0.85 plus 0.075 per year since DefiLlama first tracked the protocol, clamped to 0.85–1.15: a longer run with no token prices in more users and history.

    The basis is circulating market cap, not fully diluted value — deliberately conservative. Every row of the board prints its three factors, so any valuation on this page can be re-computed by hand from the snapshot's figures.
